Executive Summary

A digital trust bridge connecting buyers and sellers.
We hold the funds; you deliver the value.
Satisfaction guaranteed for both sides of the coin.

The Trust Deficit

The Problem

  • Buyers fear "What I ordered vs What I got"
  • Sellers fear fake alerts and chargebacks
  • No local P2P trust standard exists
  • WhatsApp/Instagram commerce is risky

The Solution

  • Automated payment escrow holding
  • Multi-step verification lifecycle
  • Logistics-integrated confirmation
  • Centralized dispute mediation

Target Market

Our primary focus is the explosion of Nigerian Social Commerce:

  • Instagram & WhatsApp Vendors
  • Freelance Developers & Designers
  • High-value gadget retailers
  • Local manufacturers & artisans

Core Tagline: "No Trust? No Problem."

System Architecture

The Tech Stack

  • Frontend: React / Next.js (SSR for SEO)
  • Backend: Node.js (Express/Fastify)
  • Database: PostgreSQL (ACID compliance)
  • Gateway: Paystack / Flutterwave Hooks
  • Messaging: Redis / RabbitMQ for events

Security & Disputes

Operational Safeguards

  • Tiered KYC: Identity verification for all high-volume sellers.
  • Mediated Disputes: Manual review of evidence for failed transactions.
  • Automated Refunds: Logic-gated reversals if seller fails to ship.
  • Compliance: AML & CTF checks integrated into the backend.
